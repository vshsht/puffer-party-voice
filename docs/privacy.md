# Privacy policy: Voice input for Puffer Party (unofficial)

_Last updated: 11 November 2026_

This extension turns your speech into answers in Krillion's Puffer Party. It has no servers or analytics, and it doesn't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

## The microphone
- **When it's used:** the extension only listens after you turn it on with the **MIC** button or **Alt+V** on `krillion.io/party`. Your browser asks your permission first.
- **When it stops:** it stops when you turn it off or leave the game.
- **What's kept:** audio and text aren't saved anywhere by the extension.

## Where speech is turned into text
- **Chrome:** the extension uses Chrome's built-in speech recognition. Chrome may send your audio to Google to transcribe it, under [Google's privacy policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy). The extension never receives or sends audio itself.
- **Firefox:** the extension runs the Vosk engine on your computer. Audio never leaves your computer, and the engine makes no network requests other than loading its own model file from inside the extension.

## What the game sees
When it's your turn, the extension types what you said into the game's answer box and presses send, so Krillion receives your answer just as if you'd typed it. The game also records how answers are entered, and it sees these as scripted (`synthetic`) input. The extension doesn't hide that.

## Permissions
The extension only runs on `https://krillion.io`. It asks for no other permissions and makes no network requests of its own.

## Contact
Questions or concerns: [open an issue on GitHub](https://github.com/vshsht/puffer-party-voice/issues).
